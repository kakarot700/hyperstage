# HyperStage Spatial Benchmark

10 deterministic spatial authoring tasks. Each is written once with absolute coordinates (**baseline**, reproducing a common agent mistake) and once with HyperStage `snapTo` anchors plus `autoFixScene` (**HyperStage**). A task passes when every contact is within 1 mm, every joint anchor pair coincides within 1 mm, the hero camera sees its subject, the goal is reachable, and the lint report is clean.
